Abstract

Learn the space, predict in it, act with detail.

Conclusion: Predicting a compact, policy-learned World representation improves control without generating pixels or futures at deployment. Current-frame features preserve the spatial detail needed for action.

Evidence: 80.85% on LIBERO-Plus, 41.91% average on RoboTwin, and 96.3% across three real-robot tasks. The 2B model trains in 9.67 hours on 8× RTX 5090. On 8× A100, it trains about 6× faster than Fast-WAM and 2× faster than π0.5.

Why it works

Predictive training organizes the World representation.

Future prediction keeps the World state coherent over time

Perturbation separation and complementary features

Change separates. Perturbation–temporal subspace overlap falls from 0.798 to 0.187; perturbation projection into the temporal subspace falls from 0.807 to 0.140.

The state resists perturbations. Paired perturbations shift zt by 0.134 versus 0.344 without prediction; cosine similarity rises from 0.932 to 0.990.

Two channels complement each other. The action probe reaches R2 = 0.927 with zt and vt together, versus 0.802 and 0.910 alone.
